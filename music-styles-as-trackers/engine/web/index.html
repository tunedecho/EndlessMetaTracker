how this works

Same engine as the offline renderer, played live. The grid is the whole song row, every step of it; each band starts a phrase and names the phrase each track plays there (with its chain transpose). Play streams the song from the cursor's row and Loop row repeats one row; edits are heard from the next bar. A step belongs to a phrase, so an edit changes that phrase everywhere it plays; Make unique here copies it first. Generate writes a fixed song from the chosen style and seed. Endless starts a run that never ends; − and + nudge the energy of the next section.
