ENDLESS META TRACKER

Music styles as tracker systems: every note and every synth voice is data. A browser tracker with its own synth engines, generative styles, per-note parameter locks, weirdness macros, and a jam mode for building songs a track at a time. Plain JavaScript, no build step, everything runs in your browser.

Open

TrackerPlay, step through, and edit songs; jam a song together track by track; turn the weirdness knobs; run Endless mode. Synth playgroundPick any instrument, move every parameter, play it from your keyboard. Parameter cubeThe tracker grid in 3-D: every parameter of every step, as a field of boxes.

Try this

  1. Open the Tracker, pick Hello Tracker, and press L to loop it.
  2. In the jam panel, add a kick, then hats; press ↻ on a track for a new take.
  3. Turn up chaos and drift; press ` to flip back to the original.
  4. Press Endless for a run that never stops; − and + steer its energy.
